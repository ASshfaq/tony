Iron Man’s missions span a vast and complex legacy, beginning with his personal battle in *Demon in a Bottle*, where Tony Stark confronts alcoholism and corporate sabotage. In *Armor Wars*, he takes on rogue tech users, even clashing with allies. *Extremis* redefines him as a biotech-enhanced hero, while *The Five Nightmares* pits him against Ezekiel Stane’s deadly innovations. He faces betrayal in *Iron Monger*, rebuilds his mind in *Stark Disassembled*, and sacrifices his memories in *World’s Most Wanted*. *The Secret Origin of Tony Stark* reveals alien ties to his birth, and *Iron Metropolitan* sees him attempt a utopian city. In *Rings of the Mandarin*, he battles magical threats, while *Execute Program* forces him to confront weaponized AI. *The Godkiller* sends him to space in massive armor, and *Industrial Revolution* challenges his tech ethics. *The Enemy Within* explores mental breakdowns, and *The Dragon Seed Saga* brings him face-to-face with ancient dragons. *Remote Control* shows his armor turned against him, and *Self-Made Man* reboots his identity. *The Mask in the Iron Man* introduces a new Iron Man during Tony’s coma, while *Manhunt* sees him framed and pursued globally. In *Believe*, he explores post-Extremis morality, and *Machinery of War* tests his bond with War Machine. *The Sons of Yinsen* challenge his legacy, *Iron Man vs. Hulk* strains friendships, *Book of the Ten Rings* dives into Mandarin lore, and *Stark Realities* traps him in a digital utopia gone wrong. Each mission reflects his evolution—from flawed genius to armored icon.
